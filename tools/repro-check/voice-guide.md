## Who I am in threads

<!-- 2-3 lines. Who is talking when you comment on an issue: your

I am a college student studying computer science who is new to making contributions. I have some experience working in large repositories, but may require time to get understand the code to make a successful change. I make contributions to improve my dev skills and to make a real impact. 

## Rules I write by

Rule: Human voice

Write the the reproduction claim like you would if you were explaining the issue to someboady.

- Wrong: "I would like to express my interest in contributing to this issue and will proceed to conduct a comprehensive reproduction."
- Right: "The crash when exporting an empty file has been an issue I've run into, so I'd like to take this issue on. I'll set up the repo and provide a reproduction repord next."

Rule: No time-bound deadlines

Do not promise any time-bound deadlines, but instead promise the next step I will take.
- Wrong: "I will have this done in two days."
- Right: "My next step is to investigate this solution and report back."

Rule: Don't pressure the maintainer

Don't ask to be assigned or push for a reply.

- Wrong: "Please assign this to me, I need to start today."
- Right: "I'd like to take this if it's still available. Happy to follow whatever process you prefer."


## Things I never post

<!-- A short list. Promises you cannot keep, tones you refuse,
shortcuts you know you reach for when tired. The skill quotes this
list back at you when a draft crosses it. -->

- Rude or impatient tones
- Robotic sounding tone (stiff, not human-like)
- Promises that are time-bound
- Overstating my experience
- Implying I could do better than previous contributors
- Copying and pasting phrasing from the issue description and pretending it's my own wording.
